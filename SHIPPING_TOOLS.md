# Durcheap Shipping Tools & Features

## Overview
Comprehensive shipping management system designed to streamline logistics for both sellers and buyers while providing competitive rates and tracking capabilities.

## Seller Shipping Tools

### 1. Shipping Calculator & Rate Comparison
**Multi-Carrier Integration**:
- USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL rate comparison
- Real-time shipping cost calculation based on:
  - Package dimensions and weight
  - Origin and destination zip codes
  - Delivery speed preferences
  - Insurance and tracking options

**Bulk Shipping Tools**:
- Batch label printing for multiple orders
- CSV import/export for shipping data
- Automated shipping rule creation by product category
- Volume discount calculations for high-volume sellers

### 2. Label Generation & Printing
**Integrated Label Creation**:
- Professional shipping labels with seller branding
- QR codes for easy tracking and returns
- Customs forms for international shipping
- Return labels automatically included

**Print Management**:
- Compatible with standard 4x6 label printers
- PDF generation for regular printers
- Mobile-friendly label viewing and printing
- Batch printing with sorting options

### 3. Inventory & Shipping Management
**Warehouse Integration**:
- Multi-location inventory tracking
- Automated shipping from nearest warehouse
- Stock level alerts and reorder points
- SKU-based shipping rules

**Order Processing Workflow**:
- Automated order import from Durcheap
- Pick list generation with optimized routing
- Packing slip creation with product details
- Shipping confirmation with tracking numbers

### 4. Shipping Analytics & Reporting
**Performance Metrics**:
- Average shipping costs by product category
- Delivery time performance by carrier
- Shipping cost as percentage of product price
- Customer satisfaction ratings for shipping

**Cost Optimization**:
- Shipping cost trends and recommendations
- Carrier performance comparison
- Zone skipping and consolidation opportunities
- Packaging optimization suggestions
