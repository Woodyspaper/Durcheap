# Durcheap Mobile App Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: July 23, 2025**

## Introduction

Durcheap ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, profile picture
- **Contact Information**: Shipping addresses, phone numbers
- **Payment Information**: Payment methods (processed securely by third-party providers)

### Usage Information
- **App Usage**: Features used, time spent, interactions
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Location Data**: Approximate location for shipping and local recommendations (with permission)

### Shopping Data
- **Purchase History**: Orders, transactions, cart contents
- **Preferences**: Wishlists, favorite categories, search history
- **Reviews**: Product reviews and ratings you submit

## How We Use Your Information

### Service Operation
- Process orders and payments
- Manage user accounts and authentication
- Provide customer support
- Send order confirmations and shipping updates

### App Enhancement
- Personalize your shopping experience
- Improve app functionality and performance
- Analyze usage patterns and trends
- Develop new features and services

### Communication
- Respond to your inquiries
- Send important service notifications
- Share promotional offers (with your consent)
- Provide customer support

## Information Sharing

### We Never Sell Your Data
We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

### Service Providers
We may share information with trusted service providers who assist us in:
- Payment processing
- Order fulfillment and shipping
- Customer support
- App analytics and improvement

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or to protect our rights and safety.

## Data Security

### Protection Measures
- Encryption of sensitive data in transit and at rest
- Secure authentication with industry-standard protocols
- Regular security audits and updates
- Limited access to personal information by authorized personnel only

### Your Responsibilities
- Keep your account credentials secure
- Use strong, unique passwords
- Log out of shared devices
- Report suspicious activity immediately

## Your Rights and Choices

### Account Management
- Update your profile information
- Manage communication preferences
- Review your order history
- Delete your account and associated data

### Data Access
- Request a copy of your personal information
- Correct inaccuracies in your data

### Marketing Communications

- Manage personalized advertising preferences

Our app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware of such collection, we will delete the information immediately.
